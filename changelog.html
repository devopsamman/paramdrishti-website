Changelog

Product Updates and Release Notes

Explore the latest Paramdrishti improvements, including feature updates, interface refinements, reporting enhancements, and performance-focused platform upgrades.

April 10, 2026

Version 1.3.0 - Performance Comparison Dashboard

This update introduces a refined performance comparison view designed to help teams evaluate projected results against actual execution more clearly. The new chart presentation improves visibility across multi-point trend data, making it easier to identify performance gaps, growth peaks, and areas that require attention. We also improved the visual balance of the reporting card, polished the chart layering, and enhanced readability so users can review key progress metrics more efficiently during daily workflow analysis.

March 28, 2026

Version 1.2.5 - Operational Growth and Reporting Update

We upgraded the operational reporting experience to provide a clearer view of growth patterns, activity movement, and overall platform performance. This release improves how chart-based summaries are displayed inside dashboard cards, with more consistent spacing, stronger visual hierarchy, and smoother interpretation of upward and downward trends. The result is a more professional and reliable reporting environment for teams that depend on quick visual insight when reviewing system activity, team output, and planning performance.

March 12, 2026

Version 1.2.0 - Customer Engagement Insight Improvements

This release enhances the way customer activity and engagement trends are presented across the platform. With improved multi-line data visualization, teams can now read interaction patterns, retention movement, and overall engagement behavior with greater clarity. We also refined the chart styling, updated the visual structure of analytics cards, and improved legibility across key reporting areas. These changes make it easier to understand shifting user behavior and turn chart-based insight into faster, more confident decision-making.
