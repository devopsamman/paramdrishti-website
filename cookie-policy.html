Transparency & Data Governance

Cookie Policy

Effective Date: February 10, 2026 | Last Updated: September 13, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how PARAMDRISHTI PRIVATE LIMITED ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") utilizes cookies, pixel tags, local storage, and related tracking technologies when you visit our website (paramdrishti.com), interact with our AI platforms, or access our online documentation.

Summary of Our Cookie Principles

We believe in transparent data practices. We do not sell user data. Cookies are deployed strictly to ensure site security, remember your user preferences, measure website stability, and analyze performance so we can provide enterprise-grade digital experiences.

1. What are Cookies and Tracking Technologies?

A cookie is a small text file containing a string of characters that is placed on your computer, tablet, or mobile device by a web server when you access a webpage. Cookies allow websites to recognize your device upon subsequent visits, keep you logged into secure areas, preserve user preferences, and help us understand which pages are most useful to visitors.

Alongside cookies, we may use related browser technologies such as:

  • Local Storage & Session Storage: HTML5 storage mechanisms that hold data within your client browser to optimize load speed and preserve state during a browsing session.
  • Web Beacons & Pixels: Transparent graphic files embedded in web pages or transactional emails used to confirm message receipt or gauge interface engagement.
  • Server Access Logs: IP address, user-agent string, referrer URL, and timestamp records collected automatically for network security and intrusion prevention.

2. Categories of Cookies We Deploy

We classify cookies into four distinct functional categories:

A. Strictly Necessary / Essential Cookies

These cookies are indispensable for the core operation of our website and client portal. They enable security features (such as Cross-Site Request Forgery / CSRF mitigation), server load balancing, user authentication, and secure session management. The website cannot function properly without these cookies, and they cannot be switched off in our systems.

B. Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies collect aggregated, anonymous data regarding how visitors navigate the site, which pages are most frequently visited, and whether error messages are encountered. We utilize these insights to measure server performance, fix broken links, and enhance user experience. Analytics cookies do not collect personally identifying details directly.

C. Functional Cookies

Functional cookies allow our website to remember choices you make (such as your preferred language, region, or UI theme settings) to provide a more tailored and continuous experience across multiple sessions.

D. Marketing & Conversion Measurement Cookies

These cookies may be set through our site by our verified advertising partners (such as Google Ads or LinkedIn Ads) to understand campaign reach and verify legitimate conversion events (such as form submissions or enterprise quote requests). They work by uniquely identifying your browser and device.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

We may integrate services provided by reputable third-party partners to support our technical infrastructure and monitor site performance:

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC): We utilize Google Analytics to understand traffic flow and geographic engagement. Google processes data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and offers an official opt-out browser add-on available at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • Cloudflare / CDN Infrastructure: We deploy content delivery networks and security proxies that place strict security cookies (e.g., bot management and DDoS protection tokens) to safeguard our infrastructure.
  • Google Fonts & Ionicons: Web font assets and iconography are loaded via privacy-preserving external CDNs to render clean typography without user profiling.

4. Cookie Lifespan & Retention

Cookies deployed on our site differ in duration:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary files that exist only while your browser session is active. Once you close your web browser, these cookies are automatically deleted.
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain stored on your device's hard drive for a predetermined period (typically ranging from 30 days up to 12 months) or until you manually clear your browser cache.

5. How You Can Control & Disable Cookies

You retain full authority over your cookie preferences. Most modern web browsers automatically accept cookies by default, but you can modify your settings to decline, block, or delete cookies at any time:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies → See all site data and permissions.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies / Manage Website Data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data.

Please note: If you choose to completely disable or reject cookies, certain essential features of our client workspace or interactive demos may not function as intended.

6. Statutory Compliance (DPDP Act 2023 & Global Standards)

This Cookie Policy complies with the provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and international data protection standards including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We ensure that any processing of tracking data adheres to principles of lawfulness, purpose limitation, and data minimization.

7. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in our technological stack, statutory requirements, or partner practices. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued navigation of the site following notice indicates your acknowledgment of the updated policy.
